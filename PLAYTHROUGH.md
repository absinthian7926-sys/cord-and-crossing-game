# Slice 1 Playthrough

This checklist covers the complete quest, The Line at the Rise, at Medium quality. Start from a clean save and allow 15 to 25 minutes for a first run.

## Before you start

1. Open the production preview.
2. Choose **New game** on the title screen.
3. Open **Pause, Settings** with Esc and confirm **Medium** graphics quality.
4. To see live laptop measurements, add `?perf=1` to the preview URL. The readout shows fps, mean and p95 frame time, 1% low fps, draw calls, triangles, quality, device pixel ratio, scene, and renderer.

To reset progress, open Esc, choose **Restart**, and confirm. Restart clears the game save but keeps graphics, audio, accessibility, and mouse settings.

## Controls

- Click the game to capture the pointer. Move the mouse to look.
- WASD moves. Shift runs. Space jumps. Ctrl or C dodges.
- LMB performs the close combo.
- Hold RMB to aim, then release to cast. Hold RMB and press LMB for the flat arc.
- Q recalls the blade. Double-tap Q stops the cast and drops the blade. Walk over a dropped blade to recover it.
- E interacts. F clears an active effect through the heelstones when prompted.
- Tab opens the quest log. Esc opens or closes the pause menu. The mouse wheel changes camera distance.
- F3 toggles the performance readout when it is available in development or with `?perf=1`.

## Quest route

### 1. Talk to Hollen

Hollen stands beside the waystation shelter, just west of the spawn point. Approach until **E Talk** appears, press E, and choose the first response.

Expected:

- The tracker advances to the brigand camp objective.
- Hollen uses the talk animation.
- The waystation has soft wood footsteps. Ambient surf and wind remain audible.

### 2. Take the frame shelter key

Follow the cart road east and uphill. The brigand camp is northeast of the road, about 450 m from the waystation, centered near `(380, -240)`. Defeat the camp leader near the fire. Approach the dropped iron ring key and press E when **E Take key** appears. Clearing every other camp enemy is optional.

Expected:

- Road brigands telegraph a raised sword before striking. Archers visibly draw before releasing arrows.
- Blade hits, buckler blocks, player hurt, footsteps, arrows, and combat music are audible.
- The quest log keeps the key after pickup and advances to the return objective.

### 3. Return to Hollen

Follow the road west to the waystation, talk to Hollen again, and choose the first response.

Expected:

- Hollen explains the heelstone pass.
- The tracker advances to the Ruin Shelf.

### 4. Unlock and cross the portico

Follow the road east to its end at the Ruin Shelf near `(600, -80)`. Approach from the road side until **E Use key** appears, then press E. Walk fully through the active screen. Stepping only partway in and backing out does not count.

Expected:

- The key remains listed as used in the quest log.
- The screen fades in with a pale ripple and remains one self-contained shimmer surface.
- A full crossing plays the portico chord, shows a brief flash, grants Strength, and loads Crossing Hollow.
- The Strength icon and timer appear above health and stamina.

### 5. Clear Crossing Hollow

Press F while the blade is In Hand and **F Clear the line** is visible. Stay still until the 1.2 s pass completes. Cast the carried line at the marked snag post about 30 m down the path. Continue through the opened gap and defeat the two Road brigands and one Brigand archer.

Expected:

- The heelstone pass animation and chime finish together. Dodging or taking damage interrupts the pass and carries nothing.
- The snag post moves exactly 2 m. Strength changes its movement speed, not its distance.
- The tracker advances only after a pass has completed, the post has moved, and all three enemies are defeated.

### 6. Return and report

Walk back through the Hollow portico screen. Follow the cart road west to the waystation and talk to Hollen.

Expected:

- The return crossing grants no new effect.
- Reporting completes the quest and attaches the dyed cloth tassel at the reachblade ring.
- Reloading keeps the completed quest and tassel.

## Visual and performance checks

- At Low, no post-processing composer is active.
- At Medium, SMAA is active. The vignette and warm grade remain High-only after the measured Medium cost fallback.
- At High, light bloom is added.
- Reduce motion removes camera shake, halves the shimmer motion, and limits the crossing flash to 35 percent opacity.
- Draw calls should remain below 250 and visible triangles below 500,000 at the waystation, Ruin Shelf, Hollow encounter, and brigand camp.
- If automatic quality fallback lowers the tier, a four-second message explains the change. A manually selected tier always wins for the current page session.

## Known issues

- The Slice 1 GLBs were generated by the deterministic code-built fallback because Blender was not installed in the implementation environment. They are compressed, self-authored CC0 assets and can be rebuilt later with the scripted Blender pipeline.
- Headless CI uses software rendering and cannot prove the 60 fps Medium target on a 2020 integrated-graphics laptop. Use the production preview with `?perf=1` for that hardware check.
